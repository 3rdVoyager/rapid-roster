About

A workspace for organizers who need fair groups — and are tired of fighting the spreadsheet.

Why it exists

Spreadsheets are great at holding names and numbers. They are terrible at holding rules like “keep these two together” and“balance skill levels” in a way you can edit without undoing half the grid.

RapidRoster is for the organizer who already knows the constraints — and wants a tool that keeps them honest when the list gets long.

Open source

RapidRoster is released under the MIT License. You can use it, fork it, and adapt it for your own events.

The product is still early. Feedback is always welcome — especially where the rules model or the review step falls short.
