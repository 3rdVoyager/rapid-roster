The problem

Manually building rosters is slow, and spreadsheets make the tradeoffs invisible.

  • schedule
    Everything fights everything

    Teammate requests, availability, roles, and skill balance all pull in different directions.

  • table
    Rules can't be seen in cells

    The spreadsheet can only hold names and numbers. The constraints live in your head.

  • replay
    One change breaks three others

    Move one person and you redo half the grid. Fairness becomes guesswork, and you never know what you traded off.

Why RapidRoster

RapidRoster lets organizers state tradeoffs as an explicit rule list — then finds placements that respect hard constraints and score best on the soft ones.

Import people and slots, set priorities, generate assignments, review what was traded off, and export when it feels right.
